## 1. Introduction
Acme SaaS is a modern platform designed to help businesses optimize their workflow. Our goal is to provide a seamless, intuitive experience that lets you focus on what really matters—growing your business.

## 2. Getting Started

### Sign Up
1. Visit our homepage.
2. Click the **"Start for Free"** button in the center of the screen or the **"Get Started"** button in the top right.
3. Enter your email address and create a secure password.
4. You will receive a confirmation email. Click the link inside to activate your account.

### Logging In
- Click **"Sign In"** at the top right of the homepage.
- Enter your credentials to access your personal dashboard.

## 3. Features

### The Dashboard
Your **Dashboard** is your command center. From here, you can see an overview of your activity, recent notifications, and quick links to key tools.

- **Activity Overview**: See a visual summary of your usage.
- **Quick Actions**: One-click buttons to start new projects or invite team members.

### Analytics
Our built-in analytics give you insights into your performance.
- View clear, easy-to-read charts.
- Filter data by date range (e.g., "Last 7 Days", "Last Month").

## 4. Managing Your Account

### Profile Settings
Go to **Settings > Profile** to update your name, email, or profile picture.

### Security
- **Change Password**: Regularly update your password for safety.
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: We recommend enabling this for an extra layer of security.

## 5. Billing & Subscriptions

### Pricing Plans
We offer flexible tiers to suit your needs:
- **Free**: For individuals just getting started.
- **Pro**: For professionals needing advanced features.
- **Enterprise**: For large teams requiring custom solutions.

### Managing Payments
- Securely add or update your credit card information in **Settings > Billing**.
- View and download past invoices for your records.

## 6. Support
Need help? We're here for you.
- **Help Center**: Browse articles and tutorials.
- **Contact Us**: Reach out to our support team directly via the "Help" widget in the bottom right corner of the dashboard.
